Your campus is part of the picture

Tech has a material footprint.

Plastic is easy to overlook when it arrives around a new component, in a delivery box, or alongside a quick meal. Small changes in how we buy, sort, and reuse can keep that material in circulation.

From lab bench to living space

Electronic parts often arrive sealed in bags, foam, and layered packaging. Keep clean packaging sorted, check local recycling guidance, and ask labs or suppliers about reusable storage.

In hostels and study spaces, refill a bottle, choose reusables, and keep food-contaminated items out of recycling. Better sorting starts with knowing what your local system accepts.
